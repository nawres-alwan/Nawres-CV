II. Research Profile

Selected Research Experience and Technical Capabilities.

The following areas reflect completed research, published work, ongoing manuscripts, implemented software, and academic projects.

Quantum Cryptography and QKD

Development and analysis of quantum cryptographic models, dynamically modulated quantum key distribution, finite-key security concepts, and secure quantum communication protocols.

Quantum-Mechanical and Hamiltonian Modelling

Mathematical and computational modelling of quantum systems, Hamiltonian dynamics, quantum simulation, and quantum-information processes.

Quantum Geometric Phases

Investigation of nonadiabatic and noncyclic geometric phases and their applications in quantum randomness, cryptography, and phase-sensitive quantum systems.

Quantum Image Processing

Design of quantum image representation and encryption models using adjacency matrices, Fourier transforms, multichannel encoding, and quantum circuits.

Chaos and Dynamical Systems

Development and analysis of high-dimensional hyperchaotic systems for encryption, pseudo-random number generation, secure information processing, and quantum-driving models.

Quantum Optimization

Research on quantum natural-gradient optimization, damping strategies, trust-region concepts, variational quantum circuits, and robust numerical training methods.

Machine Learning and AI Engineering

Development of deep-learning and computer-vision workflows for face recognition and optical character recognition, together with AI applications built using open-source models and frameworks.

Numerical and Scientific Computing

Implementation of numerical experiments, simulation algorithms, data analysis, visualization, and reproducible scientific workflows using Python and MATLAB.
