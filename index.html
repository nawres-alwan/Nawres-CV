III. Research Profile

Selected Research Experience and Technical Capabilities.

The following areas reflect completed research, published work, ongoing manuscripts, implemented software, and academic projects. Areas are ordered by relevance to quantum computing and quantum information research; items marked ongoing are not yet published.

Priority 1

Quantum Cryptography, QKD and Quantum Information Security

Development and analysis of quantum cryptographic models, dynamically modulated quantum key distribution, finite-key security analysis, quantum randomness, and secure quantum communication protocols.

Priority 2

Quantum Machine Learning, Optimization and Algorithms

Quantum natural-gradient optimization, damping strategies, trust-region concepts, variational quantum circuits, and robust numerical training methods; quantum deep-learning approaches built on quantum image representation (ongoing).

Priority 3

Quantum Image Representation, Geometric Methods and Simulation

Quantum image representation and encryption models using adjacency matrices, Fourier transforms, multichannel encoding and quantum circuits; nonadiabatic and noncyclic geometric phases; Hamiltonian modelling and quantum simulation.

Priority 4

Mathematical Foundations, Chaos Theory and Numerical Methods

Graph theory and spectral methods, high-dimensional hyperchaotic systems for encryption and pseudo-random number generation, nonlinear dynamics, numerical simulation, and reproducible scientific computing in Python and MATLAB.

Machine Learning and AI Engineering

Deep-learning and computer-vision workflows for face recognition and optical character recognition, together with AI applications built using open-source models and frameworks.
